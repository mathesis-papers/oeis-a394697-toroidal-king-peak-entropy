Abstract

Extending Liang's (2025) free-board peak-entropy argument, we prove Kotěšovec's conjecture in OEIS A394697, history revision 21 (6 May 2026; entry snapshot revision 24): the largest coefficient of the n-by-n toroidal king independence polynomial has n²th root tending to A247413 through all integers n >= 3. The same comparison gives fixed-positive-activity entropy on arbitrary growing rectangles with free, cylindrical or toroidal boundaries, explicit boundary-to-area error bounds, and equal entropy for marked-count moments. Scalar log-concavity fails infinitely often; the even maximum-packing formula and its limiting density are reproved.
